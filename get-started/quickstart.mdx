title: "Quickstart"

This quickstart takes you from an empty app to a project with real content in it: three researched company-context documents, a workstream plan, and a PRD derived from your own notes.

Budget about fifteen minutes, most of it waiting while research runs in the background.

## Prerequisites

- Figaza installed and signed in — see [Install and sign in](/get-started/install)
- Remaining AI credit on your account (new personal accounts include a one-time grant)
- The name or website of the company you work for

## 1. Create a project with company research

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new project">
    On first run you land on **Start a new project**. If you already have projects, open the project menu at the top of the sidebar and choose **New project**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the company">
    Type a URL, a company name, or a short description into the input. Figaza labels what it detected — **URL detected**, **Company name detected**, or **Description** — so you can tell how it will search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start research">
    Choose **Start research**. Figaza searches public sources and reads the company's own pages.

    This runs as a background agent. Choose **Minimize** and keep working — you can follow progress in the **Agents** section of the sidebar at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and create the project">
    When research finishes, confirm the **Project name** and choose **Create project**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You now have a project containing a `company-context/` folder with three documents:

| Document | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `company-overview` | Summary, products and services, market and positioning, business model, size and stage |
| `personas` | Three to five personas with goals, pains, and success metrics, each tagged as confirmed from evidence or inferred from industry |
| `competitor-analysis` | Three to six competitors with positioning and differentiators, plus how the company compares |

<Tip>
  Every research document ends with a **Follow-up questions for the PM** section listing what the research could not resolve. Figaza flags gaps instead of inventing facts — start there.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Prefer to start from what you already have? The same screen offers **Start with an empty project**, **Import a folder**, and **Import a git repository**. See [Create a project](/projects/create-a-project).
</Note>

## 2. Ask the assistant about your project

The chat panel sits on the right-hand side of the window.

1. Make sure the mode selector reads **Agent**.
2. Ask something that requires reading the project, for example: `Summarise our competitive position in three bullets, and tell me what evidence is missing.`

The assistant reads project documents as it works. Tool cards in the transcript show you exactly what it read — you are never guessing what it based an answer on.

## 3. Capture what you are working on

Open the **Plan** section in the sidebar and choose **Start new plan**. Give the workstream a short title, a description of what you are working toward, and a few checklist items.

If you start a new chat in a project without a plan, the assistant may propose one from your conversation. A proposal stays marked pending until you **Execute**, **Merge**, or **Reject** it — nothing replaces your plan silently. See [Workstream plans](/plans/quickstart).

## 4. Write a source document

Create the document your PRD will be derived from.

1. In the sidebar's project file tree, choose **New file**.
2. Name it something like `feature-brief.md`.
3. Write a few paragraphs about the feature you have in mind — the problem, who it is for, and roughly what you would build.

Ask the assistant to help. When it proposes edits, the editor shows them inline with **Accept** and **Reject** on each change, plus **Accept all** and **Reject all** at the top. Nothing is written to your document until you accept it. See [Reviewing AI changes](/ai/reviewing-changes).

## 5. Derive a PRD

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the derive dialog">
    With `feature-brief.md` open, choose **Derive...** from the document tab menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your outputs">
    Select **PRD (Engineers)**. You can also select **Elevator Pitch (C-Level)** and choose whether it comes out as a **Document** or a **Slide Deck**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Derive">
    Choose **Derive all**. Each derived document is written to the same folder as its source, with a link back to it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Derived documents flag missing information rather than inventing it, and they keep a link to their source so you can re-derive later when the brief changes. See [Derived documents](/derive/derived-documents).

## 6. Share the result

From the document tab menu you can:

- **Export as** PDF, DOCX, PPTX, or a Markdown text file
- **Publish to Drive** or **Publish to Notion**, which keeps the published copy in sync as you keep editing

See [Exporting documents](/share/export) and [Publishing to Drive and Notion](/share/publish).
