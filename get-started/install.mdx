Figaza is a desktop application. There is no browser version — the app stores your projects on your own machine and talks to AI providers from your device.

## Prerequisites

- A macOS, Windows, or Linux computer
- A Google account to sign in with
- An internet connection for sign-in, AI responses, and any connected sources

## 1. Download the app

Go to [figaza.com](https://figaza.com) and use the download link for your operating system.

Install it the way you install any other app on your platform: drag it to Applications on macOS, run the installer on Windows, or use the package for your Linux distribution.

## 2. Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Figaza">
    You land on the sign-in screen with a single **Sign in with Figaza** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate in your browser">
    Figaza opens your default browser to complete sign-in. Your identity is federated to Google — Figaza never sees or stores a password.

    While you finish in the browser, the app shows **Waiting for browser…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the app">
    Once you approve, the browser hands the session back to the desktop app automatically and you are signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If the browser completes but the app stays on **Waiting for browser…**, close the browser tab and press **Sign in with Figaza** again. The app listens on a local port for the hand-back, and a blocked or non-default browser can interrupt it.
</Note>

## 3. Check your plan

New personal accounts start with a one-time AI credit grant — $5.00 by default — so you can try the assistant before subscribing. When that credit is spent, AI features stop until you subscribe.

If your company holds a team subscription, your membership entitles you automatically and you will not be asked to pay.

Open **Settings → Account** at any time to see your current plan. See [Plans and billing](/settings/plans-and-billing) for how credit is allocated and what happens when it runs out.

## 4. Create your first project

You are ready to go. Continue with the [quickstart](/get-started/quickstart).

## Staying up to date

Figaza updates itself. When a new version is available you get an **Update Available** prompt with **Update Now** and **Later**.

Occasionally a version stops being supported. In that case the app shows **Update Required** and you must install the latest version to continue. See [Updates and feedback](/settings/updates-and-feedback).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sign-in says the session expired">
    Sign out from the user menu in the bottom-left corner, then sign in again. If the app reports that the session cannot be renewed, a full sign-out and sign-in is required rather than a retry.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A subscription is required to use Figaza">
    Your personal account has no active plan and no remaining free credit. Choose **Subscribe** to open checkout, or contact whoever manages your company's subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Your company's subscription is inactive">
    The app shows the company name and asks you to contact support. Billing for company plans is managed centrally — an individual member cannot reactivate it from the app.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
