The chat panel is scoped to the current project. Everything the assistant knows about your work comes from that project.

## Agent and Plan modes

The mode selector sits next to the composer.

| Mode | What it can do | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Read the project and change documents, with every change proposed for your review | You want work done |
| **Plan** | Read the project and propose a workstream plan — it cannot edit documents | You want to think, scope, or sequence |

In **Plan** mode the assistant cannot change your documents at all. It is the safe mode for exploring an idea when you do not want your drafts touched.

## Choosing a model

The model picker offers the models available to your account, grouped by what they are good for:

- **Our recommendation** — a sensible default if you have no strong preference
- **Largest context** — for reasoning over a lot of material at once
- **Lower Cost** — for routine work where you want your credit to go further
- **Strongest general capability** — for hard reasoning
- **Best for tool use & planning** — for multi-step work across many documents

If your account is limited to zero-data-retention models, the picker only lists models that qualify. See [Privacy](/settings/privacy).

## Pointing the assistant at things

- Type `@` to attach a specific project document to your message.
- Type `/` to run one of your [skills](/skills/quickstart) — a saved prompt, with any parameters it needs collected before it runs.

## Reading the transcript

The assistant shows its work as it goes:

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Read** | It opened a document |
| **Explore** / **Exploring project** | It looked around the project structure |
| **Searched for** / **Search for file** | It searched project content or filenames |
| **Web search** / **Navigated to** | It searched the web or fetched a page |
| **Modified** | It proposed a change to a document |
| **Linked** | It linked a document to your plan |
| **Plan** | It proposed or updated your workstream plan |
| **Saved version** | It checkpointed the project |
| **Background task** | It handed work off to a background agent |

Long turns collapse into an activity summary — *read 4 documents, edited 1 document, called 7 tools* — that you can expand.

When the assistant needs a decision from you, it asks directly in a question card. Pick one of the offered answers, type a custom one, or **Dismiss** the question to let it proceed without your input.

## Queue messages while it works

You do not have to wait for a turn to finish before writing your next instruction. **Add to queue** holds a message until the current turn completes; **Queued messages** lists what is waiting, with **Send now** and **Remove from queue**.

**Stop generation** ends the current turn immediately. If you close a chat tab while the assistant is still working, Figaza asks you to confirm, because closing stops the agent.

## Voice

Choose a voice language, then use **Start voice mode** to talk to the assistant instead of typing. English and Spanish are supported. The first time you use a language, Figaza downloads the speech models for it.

Speech recognition runs on your own machine — your audio is not sent anywhere for transcription.

**Stop voice mode** returns you to typing.

## Chat history

Each project keeps its chats. **Chat history** lists previous conversations with the time of the last message, and marks the ones with unread messages. **New chat** starts a fresh conversation.

Starting a new chat in a project is also what prompts the assistant to consider proposing a workstream plan — see [Plan proposals](/plans/plan-proposals).

## When something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Network or server errors">
    **There was a network problem reaching the AI service** or **The AI service returned an error** — use **Retry**. These are transient.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Session expired">
    Sign in again from the user menu. If the app says the session cannot be renewed, sign out fully and sign back in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="AI credit used up">
    AI features stop entirely rather than falling back to a cheaper model. See [AI credit and models](/ai/credit-and-models).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Selected model is not in the model catalog">
    The model is no longer offered. Pick another from the picker.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Selected model is not available under Zero Data Retention policy">
    Your account is restricted to zero-data-retention models and this one does not qualify. Pick a different model, or change the setting in **Settings → Privacy**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The AI returned an empty response">
    Retry the message. If it keeps happening on one model, try another.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
