When the assistant changes a document, the change goes into the document straight away and is held for your review. You decide whether it stays.

## The review flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="A proposal arrives">
    The document shows a **Pending AI changes** banner, and each change is marked inline where it belongs. The chat panel shows a **Pending changes** count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the changes in place">
    Because changes appear in the document rather than in a separate diff view, you read them in the context of the surrounding text — the thing you actually need to judge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Use **Accept** or **Reject** on each individual change, or **Accept all** and **Reject all** to take the whole proposal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What accept and reject actually do

Worth being precise about, because it affects what happens if you leave a review unfinished:

- The assistant's text is **written into the document immediately**. What you are reading is the new version, marked up against the last version you approved.
- **Accept** approves that text and moves your approved version forward.
- **Reject** restores the document from the last version you approved, discarding the change.

So a pending change is really in your document until you resolve it — it is not sitting in a staging area waiting for permission. If you leave a review unfinished and come back tomorrow, the document contains the assistant's text, still flagged for review.

<Tip>
  Resolve a review before you export, publish, or hand a document to someone. Anything that reads the document reads what is in it now, including changes you have not accepted yet.
</Tip>

**YOLO mode** in **Settings → AI** skips the review step altogether and accepts every AI change as it arrives — including changes from background agents and skills you are not watching.

## What review does not cover

Slide decks and dashboard documents have no inline review. When the assistant writes to one, the change lands directly and there is nothing to accept or reject.

[Version history](/projects/versions) is your undo for those — worth saving a version before you set the assistant loose on a deck you care about.

## Before every proposal, a clean point

Figaza saves a version of your project before the assistant starts a new round of changes. Whatever you had before the assistant touched anything is recoverable, even after you accept.

See [Versions and history](/projects/versions).

## Tracking outstanding work

The **Status** section at the top of the sidebar summarises what has changed and is not yet checkpointed, under **What you're working on**. When everything is settled it reads **No changes detected**.

If a document has pending AI changes, you cannot start a version comparison on it until you resolve them.

## What the assistant cannot do

- It cannot edit documents at all in **Plan** mode.
- Skills run with only the tools they were configured with; a read-only skill cannot write anything regardless of what it is asked to do. See [Authoring skills](/skills/authoring-skills).
- It works within the current project only.
