---
title: "Reviewing AI changes"
description: "How proposed edits work, and how to accept or reject them."
---

When the assistant changes a document, it proposes the change rather than making it. You decide what lands.

## The review flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="A proposal arrives">
    The document shows a **Pending AI changes** banner, and each change is marked inline where it belongs. The chat panel shows a **Pending changes** count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the changes in place">
    Because changes appear in the document rather than in a separate diff view, you read them in the context of the surrounding text — the thing you actually need to judge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide">
    Use **Accept** or **Reject** on each individual change, or **Accept all** and **Reject all** to take the whole proposal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing is written into the saved document until you accept it. Rejecting leaves the document exactly as it was.

The exception is **YOLO mode** in **Settings → AI**, which accepts every AI change automatically and skips this step — including for background agents and skills you are not watching. Your [version history](/projects/versions) still covers you, but undoing means restoring a version rather than clicking **Reject**.

## Before every proposal, a clean point

Figaza saves a version of your project before the assistant starts a new round of changes. Whatever you had before the assistant touched anything is recoverable, even after you accept.

See [Versions and history](/projects/versions).

## Tracking outstanding work

The **Status** section at the top of the sidebar summarises what has changed and is not yet checkpointed, under **What you're working on**. When everything is settled it reads **No changes detected**.

If a document has pending AI changes, you cannot start a version comparison on it until you resolve them.

## What the assistant cannot do

- It cannot edit documents at all in **Plan** mode.
- Skills run with only the tools they were configured with; a read-only skill cannot write anything regardless of what it is asked to do. See [Authoring skills](/skills/authoring-skills).
- It works within the current project only.
