---
title: "Quickstart: your first AI conversation"
description: "Ask the assistant about your project, have it draft a section, and review every change before it lands."
---

The assistant in Figaza is not a chat window that happens to sit next to your documents. It reads your project, works with tools you can see, and proposes changes you approve one at a time.

This quickstart takes about five minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A project with at least one document in it — see [Create a project](/projects/create-a-project)
- Remaining AI credit on your account

## 1. Ask something that requires reading the project

Open the chat panel on the right. Make sure the mode selector reads **Agent**, and ask:

```text
What does this project already say about our target users, and what is missing?
```

As the assistant works, tool cards appear in the transcript — **Read**, **Explore**, **Searched for**, and so on. Each card names the document or query involved, so you can see exactly what the answer is based on.

<Tip>
  Type `@` in the composer to point the assistant at a specific document instead of letting it search.
</Tip>

## 2. Ask it to draft something

```text
Draft a "Success metrics" section for feature-brief.md, based on the personas document.
```

The assistant proposes an edit rather than making one. In the editor you get a **Pending AI changes** banner, and every change is marked inline.

## 3. Review the change

Work through the proposal:

- **Accept** or **Reject** each individual change
- **Accept all** or **Reject all** to decide the whole proposal at once

The assistant's text is already in the document — **Reject** puts back the last version you approved. See [Reviewing AI changes](/ai/reviewing-changes).

## 4. Plan before you build

Switch the mode selector to **Plan** and ask:

```text
We want to add usage-based pricing. What should I work through first?
```

In **Plan** mode the assistant cannot edit your documents. It can only read the project and propose a workstream plan — so you get thinking, not edits. Switch back to **Agent** when you are ready to write.

## 5. Hand off something long

If the answer is going to take a while — research, a sweep across many documents — ask for it as a background task, or create one from the **Agents** section of the sidebar. It runs on its own while you carry on. See [Background agents](/ai/background-agents).
