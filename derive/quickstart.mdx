---
title: "Quickstart: derive a PRD"
description: "Turn one source document into the artifacts your engineers and executives each need."
---

You wrote the thinking once. Your engineers need it as requirements, your leadership needs it as a pitch, and neither wants the other's document.

Deriving generates those artifacts from a source document you own, links them back to it, and keeps them updatable when the source changes.

## Prerequisites

- A project with a source document in it — a brief, a spec, or an initiative document
- Remaining AI credit

## 1. Open the source document

Open the document your artifacts should be based on. The richer it is, the less gets flagged as missing.

## 2. Open the derive dialog

From the document tab menu, choose **Derive...**. The dialog confirms which document you are deriving from.

## 3. Choose your outputs

| Output | Written for |
| --- | --- |
| **PRD (Engineers)** | Problem, goals and non-goals, user stories, functional and non-functional requirements, technical considerations, open questions |
| **Elevator Pitch (C-Level)** | One-liner, problem, solution, market opportunity, business model, the ask |

For the elevator pitch, choose a **Format**: **Document** or **Slide Deck**.

You can select both outputs and generate them together.

## 4. Pick a model and derive

Choose a **Model** if you want something other than the default, then **Derive all**. The dialog shows progress and counts down as each document completes.

## 5. Read the gaps first

Both outputs end with a section listing what was missing — **Open Questions & Missing Information** in the PRD, **Missing Information** in the pitch.

<Warning>
  Derived documents never invent facts to fill a hole. If your source did not say what the success metric is, the PRD says the success metric is undefined. Those sections are the most useful part of the output — they are your to-do list.
</Warning>

## 6. Find your documents

Each derived document is saved in the same folder as its source, named with the date and time it was generated, and linked back to the source.

To see everything derived from a document, right-click it in the sidebar and choose **Derived documents**.

## 7. Keep them current

When the source changes, choose **Update derived...** from the source document's tab menu. Figaza updates the sections affected by what changed and leaves the rest alone. See [Derived documents](/derive/derived-documents).
