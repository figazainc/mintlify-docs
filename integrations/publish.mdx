---
title: "Publishing to Drive and Notion"
description: "Push a document out to Google Drive or Notion, and keep the published copy up to date automatically."
---

Publishing is the outbound half of your Google Drive and Notion connections. It creates a copy of a Figaza document in Drive or Notion — and remembers the link, so future changes update that same copy instead of piling up duplicates.

Where an [integration](/integrations/overview) brings data *in* on a schedule, publishing pushes a document *out* and keeps it current.

## Prerequisites

- [Google Drive](/sources/google-drive) or [Notion](/sources/notion) connected in **Sources**

Connecting is a one-time step, and the same connection serves both directions: importing pages into a project, and publishing documents back out.

## Publish to Google Drive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Publish to Drive">
    From the document tab menu, choose **Publish to Drive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a destination folder">
    Browse from **My Drive** to the folder you want, then choose **Publish here**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

What arrives in Drive depends on the document:

- **Documents** upload as Markdown and are converted into a Google Doc.
- **Slide decks** are rendered and uploaded as a native Google Slides file.

## Publish to Notion

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Publish to Notion">
    From the document tab menu, choose **Publish to Notion**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a parent page">
    Search your pages or choose **Workspace root**, then **Publish here**. Your document is created as a page under it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automatic re-sync

Once a document is published, Figaza keeps the published copy current. When your project reaches a new checkpoint, any document with a published copy is pushed again.

Two things follow from that:

- Re-sync happens on checkpoints, not on every keystroke, so your colleagues see coherent versions rather than half-finished sentences.
- Unchanged documents are never re-pushed, so publishing many documents does not mean constant churn in Drive or Notion.

Publishing the same document again updates the existing file or page. It never creates a second one.

## When sync has a problem

Figaza tells you what went wrong and offers the action that fixes it:

| Message | What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lost access to …** | The connection was revoked or expired | Reconnect the source in **Sources** |
| **The … copy was deleted** | The file or page no longer exists | **Re-export to …** to recreate and relink it |
| **… is busy and will retry automatically** | You hit a rate limit | Nothing — it retries on its own |
| **Couldn't reach …** | A network problem | Check your connection |

## Stop syncing a document

**Remove … link** disconnects the document from its published copy. The copy in Drive or Notion is left exactly where it is — nothing is deleted on the other side — and the document simply stops being updated until you publish it again.

## Publishing is not importing

The two directions are independent. Importing a Notion page and editing it in Figaza does not change the Notion page — publishing is what pushes work out, and it is always something you choose to do.
