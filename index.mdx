---
title: "Figaza for product managers"
description: "A product development environment where your documents, your plan, and an AI that knows your project all live in one place."
---

Figaza is a desktop app for product managers. It keeps the documents you write, the plan you are working to, and the sources you pull from in a single project — and gives an AI assistant full context on all of it.

Unlike a chat window bolted onto a notes app, the assistant in Figaza reads your project, proposes edits you review line by line, and can run longer jobs in the background while you keep working.

## What you can do

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Write with an assistant that has read everything" icon="pen-to-square" href="/ai/quickstart">
    Ask questions about your project, draft sections, and accept or reject every proposed change.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep a living workstream plan" icon="list-check" href="/plans/quickstart">
    Track what you are focused on now, let the assistant propose a plan from your conversation, and review what past workstreams delivered.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Derive the documents your audience needs" icon="wand-magic-sparkles" href="/derive/quickstart">
    Turn one source document into a PRD for engineers or an elevator pitch for executives — as a document or a slide deck.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bring your existing material in" icon="folder-open" href="/sources/quickstart">
    Import from your filesystem, Notion, and Google Drive, or have the assistant build a live data pipeline from another tool.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Share and publish" icon="share-nodes" href="/share/export">
    Export to PDF, DOCX, PPTX, or Markdown, and publish back to Google Drive or Notion with automatic re-sync.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Teach it your way of working" icon="graduation-cap" href="/skills/quickstart">
    Save prompts as skills you run with a slash command, and let agents call them too.
  </Card>
</Columns>
