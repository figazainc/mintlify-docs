---
title: "Quickstart: your first workstream plan"
description: "Capture what you are focused on, work the checklist, and close the workstream with a record of what changed."
---

A workstream plan answers one question: *what am I working on right now, in this project?* It survives the interruptions, the context switches, and the week you spent somewhere else.

This quickstart takes about five minutes.

## Prerequisites

- A project open in Figaza
- The **Plan** section visible in the sidebar

## 1. Start a plan

If the project has no active plan, the **Plan** section reads *No active workstream plan for this project.* Choose **Start new plan**.

Fill in:

- **Title** — a short label, a few words. This is how the workstream shows up in history later.
- **Description** — what you are working toward, in a sentence or two.

## 2. Add checklist items

Add the things this workstream has to get through. Keep them concrete — *Interview three enterprise customers* rather than *Do research*.

Each item carries a status you cycle as you go: **pending**, **in progress**, **blocked**, or **done**.

## 3. Decide who owns each item

Every item has an owner: **Human** or **AI**. Click the owner marker to switch it.

That choice is not decoration. When a plan has AI-owned items that are not finished, the plan gets an **Execute** button — and choosing it sets the assistant to work on exactly those items, leaving your own alone.

## 4. Work the plan

As you work, update item statuses. Mark something **blocked** when it is waiting on someone else, and clear the flag when it unblocks.

If an item can only start after another one, open **Edit dependencies** and add the item it needs. The plan then shows **Needs: …** on the dependent item.

## 5. Let the assistant keep the story straight

Figaza keeps a **Plan progress** summary against your plan — a running narrative of what has actually changed in the project since the workstream started. It refreshes on its own when you open the project and the summary has gone stale.

Expand **Plan progress** to read the preview, or **Open...** for the full summary. See [Progress and history](/plans/progress-and-history).

## 6. End the workstream

When the work is done, choose **End**. The plan moves to **Ended workstreams** in **History**, where it stays as a record of what that stretch of work covered.

Only one workstream is active per project at a time, so ending one is what makes room for the next.
