Figaza uses AI on every meaningful action, so your plan is really a question of how much AI credit you have. See [AI credit and models](/ai/credit-and-models) for what consumes it.

## The free tier

A new personal account gets a one-time credit grant — by default $5 — so you can try the product properly before paying.

It is genuinely one-time. It does not refill monthly, and an account that has held a subscription before does not get another grant after cancelling.

## Subscribing

When you have no active plan, Figaza shows **A subscription is required to use Figaza** with a **Subscribe** button. Choosing it opens checkout in your browser.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a plan">
    The plan selection page lists the current plans and prices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Complete checkout in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the app">
    Figaza shows **Waiting for subscription to activate…** and unlocks once payment is confirmed. If it seems stuck, **Check again** re-checks your status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A subscription gives you a monthly AI allowance proportional to your plan, which resets each month.

## Company subscriptions

If your organisation holds a company subscription, every active member is entitled through it. There is nothing for you to buy, and **Settings → Account** shows your plan as managed by that company.

When a company subscription lapses, Figaza tells you which company is affected and points you at support. An individual member cannot reactivate a company plan from the app — that has to happen wherever your organisation manages its billing.

## Managing your subscription

**Settings → Account → Manage subscription** takes you to billing, where you can change plan, update payment details, or cancel.

## When access stops

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **A subscription is required to use Figaza** | No active plan and no remaining free credit |
| **AI credit used up** | You are subscribed, but this period's allowance is spent |
| **Your company's subscription is inactive** | Company billing needs attention |

In all three cases your projects, documents, and history are untouched and stay on your machine. It is the AI features that stop, not the app.

<Note>
  Figaza never quietly switches you to a cheaper model when credit runs low. AI stops and tells you, so you are never comparing work produced at two different quality levels without knowing it.
</Note>
