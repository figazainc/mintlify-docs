Figaza is a desktop app. Your projects, documents, and history live on your computer, not on a server.

## Where your work lives

| Data | Where it is |
| --- | --- |
| Documents and project history | On your machine |
| Chat sessions and agent transcripts | On your machine |
| Background tasks and their output | On your machine |
| Workstream plans and history | On your machine |
| Credentials for connected services | In your operating system's credential store |

Nothing is uploaded unless you ask for it. [Publishing](/share/publish) sends a document to Drive or Notion; [remote sync](/share/sync) pushes project history to a Git remote you own; [integrations](/sources/integrations) call the services you set them up for. Each is something you set up deliberately.

## What goes to AI providers

Using AI means sending the relevant part of your project to a model provider — that is unavoidable for a feature that reads your documents. What is sent is scoped to what the request needs: the documents the assistant reads, the conversation, and, for a skill, the document context you configured.

Voice input is the exception: speech recognition runs locally on your machine, so your audio is not sent anywhere to be transcribed.

## The two controls

Both are in **Settings → Privacy**.

### Share anonymous usage data

*Helps us improve Figaza by understanding which features are used. No message content, file contents, or personal documents are ever collected.*

This covers which features get used, not what you wrote in them. Turn it off if you would rather send nothing at all.

### Use only Zero Data Retention models

*Limits AI models and routing to endpoints with a zero data retention policy.*

With it on, the model picker only offers models whose providers do not retain the content of requests, and selecting a non-qualifying model is refused rather than quietly allowed.

The trade-off is choice: some capable models do not offer a zero-retention endpoint and will disappear from your picker. If your organisation has a policy about model data retention, this is the setting that enforces it.

## Narrowing what agents can see

Beyond these switches, you control AI access at the level of individual skills. A skill can be limited to specific documents and given read-only tools, so it never sees or touches anything outside its remit. See [Authoring skills](/skills/authoring-skills).

## Deleting things

Deleting a project permanently removes its files and history from your machine. If the project was synced to a Git remote, the remote copy is not deleted — remove that separately at your Git host.
