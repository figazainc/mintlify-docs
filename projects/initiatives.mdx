An initiative is a body of work that spans more than one document — a migration, a launch, a quarter's theme. Where a [workstream plan](/plans/quickstart) is about what you are focused on *right now*, an initiative is the longer-running thing that work rolls up into.

Initiatives live in the **Initiatives** section of the sidebar.

## Create an initiative

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Choose the **+** in the Initiatives section header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Give it a title that names the outcome — for example `Q3 Platform Migration`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the initial state">
    Pick **Not started**, **In progress**, **Blocked**, or **Done**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Initiatives can also come from the assistant, or be promoted from a document you are already writing.

## State and risk

Each initiative carries two independent signals:

| Signal | Values | What it answers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **State** | Not started, In progress, Blocked, Done | Where is the work? |
| **Risk** | On track, At risk, Off track | How is it going? |

Keeping them separate is the point: work can be firmly in progress and off track at the same time, and that is exactly the thing worth surfacing before a review.

State changes are recorded over time, so an initiative carries its own history of when it moved between states.

## Linked documents

An initiative lists the documents that belong to it, so the brief, the PRD, the research notes, and the pitch are one click apart. Links follow documents through renames and moves.

## Archiving

Finished or abandoned initiatives can be archived. Archived initiatives collapse into an **Archived** group at the bottom of the section, and can be brought back with **Unarchive**.

Archive rather than delete when the work is done — the record of what happened stays useful long after the initiative closes.
