Figaza ships with a set of skills that power features you already use. They appear under **Built-in skills** in **Settings → Skills**, marked with a **Built-in** badge.

## What ships

| Skill | Alias | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Explore project** | `explore-project` | Read-only exploration: surveys the project, finds and reads relevant documents, and returns a cited summary. It has no write access at all. |
| **Onboarding research** | `onboarding-research` | Researches a company from public sources and produces the company overview, personas, and competitor analysis. Runs when you [create a project with research](/projects/create-a-project). |
| **Derive PRD** | `derive-prd` | Produces a PRD for engineers from a source document. See [Derived documents](/derive/derived-documents). |
| **Derive elevator pitch** | `derive-elevator-pitch` | Produces an elevator pitch for executives, as a document or a slide deck. |
| **Project briefing** | `project-briefing` | Synthesises the whole project into a briefing. See [Project briefing](/derive/project-briefing). |
| **Setup integration** | `setup-integration` | Walks you through connecting an external tool and collecting its credential. See [The /setup-integration skill](/integrations/setup-integration). |
| **Install integration** | `install-integration` | Tests, installs, and verifies the integration. Called by the setup skill rather than by you — see [The /setup-integration skill](/integrations/setup-integration). |

Two of them are deliberately one-sided: **Setup integration** is for you to run and agents cannot invoke it, and **Install integration** is for agents and does not appear in your slash menu.

## Running one yourself

The rest are available in the chat composer under `/`, the same as your own skills. Running `/project-briefing` directly is equivalent to using the briefing dialog.

## Customising a built-in skill

Choose **Customize** on a built-in skill. You get an editable copy — with the same alias — saved at whichever scope you choose. Your version is what runs from then on.

Everything is editable: the prompt, the document context, and which tools the skill may use.

To go back to the shipped behaviour, delete your customised version. The hint in the editor says as much: *Delete your customized version to restore the built-in default.*

## When to customise

The built-in derive skills are a good example. If your organisation's PRDs need a specific section — a compliance review, a rollout plan, a link to your tracker — customise **Derive PRD** once and every PRD you derive from then on includes it.

<Tip>
  Customise at **This project** scope when the change is specific to one product's conventions, and at **All projects** scope when it reflects how you work everywhere.
</Tip>
